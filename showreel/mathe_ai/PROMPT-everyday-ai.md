# Prompt: mathe_ai, "AI για κάθε μέρα" (Lesson 01)

The "Anyone can" EffectCraft prompt, re-cut for mathe_ai: a 30-second lesson that teaches Greek
students how to use AI in everyday life. "Μάθε" is Greek for "learn", so the brand reads "learn AI".

```text
<direction>
A 30-second, 16:9 lesson-promo for mathe_ai, aimed at Greek students (13–18),
with Greek voice-over and music. The goal is to teach, not only to impress. By
the end a student knows how to ask AI properly, three rules for using it, what
they can use it for every day, and two cautions. The tone is encouraging,
practical and a little funny, never preachy. The wordplay at the heart of it:
"μάθε" is Greek for "learn", so mathe_ai reads "μάθε AI", "learn AI". The
film ends by turning the Greek "μάθε AI" into the "mathe_ai" wordmark.

Keep the energy of "Anyone can": heavy type slamming in on the beat, hard cuts
on downbeats, sticker-like collage, a technical HUD over everything. But dress it
in school materials: paper grounds are a Greek school notebook (τετράδιο) with
faint cobalt ruled lines every 48 px and a vermilion margin line at x=150. Dark
grounds are graph paper or a chalkboard.

Type, all with full Greek support:
- Commissioner (Black, ExtraBold, Medium) for every Greek headline and UI label.
- JetBrains Mono for the HUD, typing, prompts and code.
- Geist Bold only for the Latin "mathe_ai" wordmark.
Greek typography rules: all-caps text drops the accents (ΚΑΘΕ ΜΕΡΑ, not ΚΆΘΕ
ΜΈΡΑ). Lowercase keeps them. The Greek question mark is ";", quotes are «…»,
class names use the keraia (Β΄ Λυκείου), and school maths writes division as ":".

Palette (mathe_ai brand): ink #0B0B0C, paper #F2EFE8, vermilion #FF4B1F (the
underscore, the "blank", every accent), cobalt #2E4BFF (structure, checks, ruled
lines), highlighter yellow #F8DD3F, plus UI greys (panel #1B1B20, line #2E2E35,
grey #8E8E98).
Motion language: "snap" eases (very fast start, long soft settle), slams that
scale in from 120–175% to 100%, motion blur on everything that moves (180°
shutter), beat pulses driven by expressions. The vermilion underscore of
mathe_ai is the recurring cursor: it blinks, stretches and wipes between scenes.
</direction>

<structure>
1920x1080, 60 fps, exactly 30 s, 128 BPM: 16 bars of 4/4 (bar 1.875 s,
beat 0.469 s). Every cut, entrance and hit lands on a beat or a subdivision.

HUD (always on top): 30 px corner brackets inset 40 px. A top-left black tag
"mathe_ai · ΜΑΘΗΜΑ 01". A top-right black tag with the section name (below).
A live timecode "00:00:SS:FF · 60 FPS" bottom-left. "128 BPM ■■□□ ΜΕΤΡΟ 03/16"
bottom-right, where the squares fill on each beat. A 3 px vermilion progress
line along the bottom fills over the 30 s. The plain HUD marks switch between
ink and paper so they stay readable on each ground.

01 — ΚΑΘΕ ΜΕΡΑ (bars 1-2)
- Beat 1: notebook paper. A short school-bell "ding". "ΚΑΘΕ" in ink, 300 px,
  slams in from 122% with a -4° tilt.
- Beat 2: cut to vermilion. "ΜΕΡΑ" in paper, 420 px, rises into place.
- Beat 3: cut to ink. "ΜΕ" in yellow, 420 px, slams from 140% at 7°, then
  shrinks and spins out.
- Beat 4: a 3x3 mosaic of everyday-student tiles pops in on 16th notes,
  scattered order, each tile scaling up from 0 with a ±9° twist (633x353 tiles,
  10 px ink gutters):
  1 vermilion: "ΜΑΘΗΜΑΤΙΚΑ" with x², π and √ floating
  2 notebook: a vocabulary card flipping "apple ↔ μήλο" on the beat
  3 ink: "ΠΑΝΕΛΛΗΝΙΕΣ" with a day counter ticking down
  4 notebook: an essay page "ΕΚΘΕΣΗ" with yellow highlighter sweeping across lines
  5 ink: a dot grid
  6 cobalt: a weekly study timetable (Δευ Τρι Τετ Πεμ Παρ) with blocks dropping
    in on the beat
  7 vermilion: a chat bubble with bouncing typing dots that resolves into «;»
  8 ink: Python typing print("γεια σου!")
  9 notebook: a quiz card with options α β γ δ, where γ gets a cobalt check
- Bar 2 downbeat: "AI" lands over the whole mosaic. Commissioner Black, about
  620 px, paper fill, 14 px ink outline, vermilion extrusion offset +20/+20.
  It slams from 175% at -9°, settles near -3°, and pulses +5% on every beat.

02 — ΧΩΡΙΣ ΠΑΠΑΓΑΛΙΑ (bar 3)
- Ink ground with a faint 60 px grid. "χωρίς" types in, mono bold 66 px, with
  a vermilion block caret blinking on the 8ths.
- Beat 2: "ΠΑΠΑΓΑΛΙΑ" (rote learning) in vermilion, about 210 px, slams from
  130% with a cobalt chromatic copy snapping into register.
- Beat 3: a paper bar tilted -7° strikes through it, left to right in 0.13 s,
  and 14 dots (cobalt, paper, yellow, vermilion) pop and drift outward.
- Beat 4: the whole group tilts to -6° and starts to fall.

03 — ΡΩΤΑ (bars 4-5)
- Notebook paper. On the left, 130 px, each word rising with a vertical squash:
  "ΡΩΤΑ" (ink, bar 4 beat 1) and "ΣΩΣΤΑ." (vermilion, beat 3).
- On the right, a brutalist chat card (800x440, white #FBFAF7, 4 px ink outline,
  solid ink offset shadow) slides in from the right with a 6° tilt. Its header
  shows the mathe_ai wordmark (vermilion underscore) and a "ΝΕΑ ΣΥΖΗΤΗΣΗ" pill.
  The prompt types in mono at about 60 characters a second, with a vermilion caret:
  «εξήγησέ μου βήμα-βήμα πώς λύνω την εξίσωση 2x + 5 = 17. είμαι στη Β΄
  λυκείου. μη μου πεις μόνο την απάντηση.»
  Footer: a "+" button and a vermilion round send button with a paper arrow.
- Bar 5: a white pointer glides in and clicks send on beat 3, and the button
  squashes. The underscore in the card's wordmark shoots out and stretches into
  a vermilion line that wipes the frame to ink by bar 6.

04 — ΒΗΜΑ-ΒΗΜΑ (bars 6-7), ink graph paper: the AI's worked answer as kinetic maths
- Bar 6 beat 1: "2x + 5 = 17" types in large, centred.
- Beat 2: a label pops «αφαιρώ 5 και από τα δύο μέλη». The "+5" lifts, arcs over
  the equals sign and lands as "−5" beside 17.
- Beat 3: "17 − 5" collapses into "12", giving "2x = 12".
- Bar 7 beat 1: a label pops «διαιρώ και τα δύο μέλη με 2». The "2" detaches from
  "2x" and drops beside 12 as ": 2".
- Beat 2: "12 : 2" collapses to "6", and "x = 6" gets a vermilion box and check.
- Beat 3: «επαλήθευση: 2·6 + 5 = 17 ✓» types small underneath.
- Beat 4: a follow-up chip slides in, «γιατί αφαιρώ το 5;», and a reply bubble
  answers «για να μείνει μόνο του το 2x.»

05 — 3 ΚΑΝΟΝΕΣ (bars 8-10): one rule per bar, each a full-frame card
Each new card slides up like a notebook page turning on the downbeat. A giant
outlined number sits behind the rule, and an example prompt types on beats 2-4.
- Bar 8, vermilion: "1" and "ΔΩΣΕ ΠΛΑΙΣΙΟ." (ink, 170 px).
  Chip: «είμαι στη Β΄ λυκείου και γράφω διαγώνισμα την Παρασκευή»
- Bar 9, cobalt: "2" and "ΖΗΤΑ ΕΞΗΓΗΣΗ." (paper).
  Chip: «εξήγησέ το μου σαν να είμαι 12 χρονών»
- Bar 10, notebook: "3" and "ΕΛΕΓΞΕ ΤΗΝ ΑΠΑΝΤΗΣΗ." (ink, two lines).
  Chip: «από πού το ξέρεις; δώσε μου την πηγή.» A cobalt check stamp hits on beat 4.

06 — ΠΑΝΤΟΥ (bars 11-12), ink ground
- Headline "ΣΤΟ ΣΧΟΛΕΙΟ." (bar 11), then "ΚΑΙ ΠΑΝΤΟΥ." (bar 12), in paper with a
  vermilion offset.
- A strip of use-case cards whips in from the right and scrolls, a new card
  popping every 8th note. Each card has an icon, a label and a mono example prompt:
  bar 11 (school):
    Περίληψη σημειώσεων: «κάνε μου περίληψη σε 5 κουκκίδες»
    Κουίζ για το διαγώνισμα: «φτιάξε μου 10 ερωτήσεις πολλαπλής επιλογής»
    Ιδέες για την έκθεση: «δώσε μου 3 επιχειρήματα υπέρ και κατά»
    Βοήθεια με Python: «γιατί βγάζει error ο κώδικάς μου;»
  bar 12 (life):
    Εξάσκηση στα Αγγλικά: «μίλα μου μόνο στα αγγλικά για 5 λεπτά»
    Email στον καθηγητή: «γράψε ένα ευγενικό email για παράταση»
    Πρόγραμμα διαβάσματος: «μοίρασε 4 μαθήματα σε 5 μέρες»
    Μαγείρεμα: «τι φτιάχνω με αυγά, ντομάτα και φέτα;»

07 — ΠΡΟΣΟΧΗ (bars 13-14), notebook paper
- Bar 13: "ΤΟ AI ΚΑΝΕΙ ΛΑΘΗ." An answer bubble types «Ο Παρθενώνας χτίστηκε
  το 1821.» On beat 3 a vermilion wavy underline draws under it and an ✗ stamp
  hits. On beat 4 the correction «447–438 π.Χ.» slides in with a cobalt check.
- Bar 14: "ΤΑ ΠΡΟΣΩΠΙΚΑ ΣΟΥ ΜΕΝΟΥΝ ΔΙΚΑ ΣΟΥ." (two lines). A form card with
  fields ΑΜΚΑ, Τηλέφωνο, Διεύθυνση and Κωδικοί: ink redaction bars wipe across
  them on the 8ths of beats 2-4, and a padlock snaps shut on beat 4.

08 — ΜΑΘΕ AI (bars 15-16), ink ground
- Bar 15 beat 1: "μάθε AI." (paper, Commissioner Black, 300 px, lowercase) slams in.
- Beats 3-4: on 8th notes the letters transliterate in place, μ→m, ά→a, θ→th
  (one glyph splits into two), ε→e and AI→ai. The space becomes the blinking
  vermilion underscore.
- Bar 16 beat 1: the "mathe_ai" wordmark locks in (Geist Bold, paper, vermilion
  underscore).
- Beat 2: the tagline types in Commissioner: «Μάθε AI. Για κάθε μέρα.»
- Beat 3: the underscore blinks once. End on this frame.
</structure>
```

# Prompt: "Anyone can", the EffectCraft edition

The complete video, written as a prompt that can regenerate it.

```text
<direction>
A 20-second, 16:9 promo with voice-over and music. The message: anyone can make
pro motion graphics without After Effects. You describe it, Claude writes the
script, and EffectCraft (the open-source After Effects alternative) builds and
renders every frame. Bold, sticker-like collage energy: heavy type that slams in
on the beat, hard cuts on downbeats, a few graphic wipes, and a technical HUD
running over everything.

Type: Archivo Black for all display words, Instrument Serif Italic as the accent,
JetBrains Mono for the HUD, typing and code, Inter for UI labels.
Palette: paper #EFEBE3, card white #FBFAF7, ink #121214, red #EF4D3A,
orange #FA6B21, blue #2741F5, yellow #F8DD3F, plus UI greys (panel #1B1B20,
line #2E2E35, grey #8E8E98) and two label colours (green #3DD68C, purple #9C6BFF).
Motion language: "snap" eases (very fast start, long soft settle), slams that
scale in from 120-175% down to 100%, motion blur on everything that moves
(180° shutter), and small beat pulses driven by expressions.
</direction>

<structure>
1920x1080, 60 fps, exactly 20 s, 132 BPM: 11 bars of 4/4 (bar 1.818 s,
beat 0.4545 s). Every cut, entrance and hit lands on a beat or a subdivision.

HUD (always on top): 30 px corner brackets inset 40 px. A top-left black tag
"ANYONE CAN · EFFECTCRAFT". A top-right black tag with the section name
("01 — HOOK" … "07 — ANYONE CAN"). A live timecode "00:00:SS:FF · 60 FPS"
bottom-left. "132 BPM ■■□□ BAR 03/11" bottom-right, where the squares fill
on each beat. A 3 px red progress line along the bottom fills over the 20 s.
The plain HUD marks switch between ink and paper so they stay readable on
each section's background.

01 HOOK (bars 1-2)
- Beat 1: paper ground. "ANYONE" in ink, 300 px, slams in from 122% with a -4° tilt.
- Beat 2: cut to red. "CAN" in paper, 470 px, rises 140 px into place.
- Beat 3: cut to ink. "MAKE" in yellow, 390 px, slams from 140% at 7°, then
  shrinks and spins out.
- Beat 4: a 3x3 mosaic of style tiles pops in on 16th notes, scattered order,
  each tile scaling up from 0 with a ±9° twist (633x353 tiles, 10 px ink gutters):
  1 orange: "MOTION" (ink) and "designer" (serif italic, paper) sliding opposite ways
  2 paper: a huge orange italic "Aa", a spinning orange triangle, an ink ring
    pulsing on the beat
  3 ink: a wireframe globe (rotating paper longitudes, red latitudes)
  4 paper: "ways to get from A to B." with four easing curves drawing on, and a
    red dot easing along a track
  5 ink: a dot grid
  6 blue: falling confetti and a paper disc pulsing on the beat
  7 red: a two-digit frame counter ticking every frame, with "FRAMES"
  8 ink: concentric red and paper rings rippling outward
  9 paper: a five-bar chart (blue, red, ink, yellow, orange) re-shuffling every beat
- Bar 2 downbeat: "THIS" lands over the whole mosaic. 540 px paper letters,
  a 14 px ink outline, a red extrusion offset +20/+20. It slams from 175% at -9°,
  settles near -3°, and pulses +5% on every following beat.

02 WITHOUT (bar 3)
- Ink ground with a faint 60 px grid. "without" types in, mono bold 66 px,
  with a red block caret blinking on the 8ths.
- Beat 2: "AFTER / EFFECTS" in red, 250 px, two lines, slams from 130% with a
  blue chromatic copy snapping into register. A soft red glow appears behind it.
- Beat 3: a paper bar tilted -7° wipes left to right across it (a strike-through)
  in 0.13 s, and 14 dots (blue, paper, yellow, red) pop around it and drift outward.
- Beat 4: the whole group tilts to -6° and starts to fall.

03 DESCRIBE (bars 4-5)
- Paper ground with a faint dot grid. On the left, 130 px, each word rising
  with a vertical squash: "JUST" (ink, bar 4 beat 1), "DESCRIBE" (red, beat 3),
  "IT." (ink, beat 4).
- On the right, a brutalist chat card (800x440, white, 4 px ink outline, solid
  ink offset shadow) slides in from the right with a 6° tilt on bar 4's downbeat.
  Header: a small red spinning 12-point burst, "Claude", and a "NEW CHAT" pill.
  The prompt types at about 66 characters a second in mono, with a red caret:
  "make a 20-second motion graphics video in EffectCraft that shows what an
  incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel. go all out."
  Footer: a "+" button and a red round send button with a paper arrow.
- Bar 5: a white mouse pointer glides in and clicks send on beat 3. The button
  squashes and a red ripple expands. A red 12-point burst grows out of the
  button, spins, rushes to the centre and floods the whole frame red by bar 6.

04 SCRIPT (bar 6), red ground
- Beat 1: an ink 12-point burst pops in and spins, and "CLAUDE" (ink, 236 px)
  slides in beside it.
- Beat 2: "WRITES THE" (ink, 150 px) rises.
- Beat 3: "SCRIPT." (paper, 236 px, ink offset shadow) slams from 150%.
- Beat 4: a dark app window pops up small in the bottom-right corner, then
  accelerates to fill the frame, straightening from -6°, with heavy motion blur.

05 BUILD (bars 7-8), a dark EffectCraft-style interface
- Traffic-light dots and the title "anyone-can.ecproj — EffectCraft".
- Code panel: 16 lines of the build script type in, one per 8th note, coloured
  by role (grey comment, paper code, yellow strings, orange keyframe values,
  green expression), starting "// anyone-can.jsx: written by Claude".
- Viewer panel ("Composition: Main"): the live hook mosaic with THIS, looping.
- Timeline panel: ruler ticks. Layer rows land one per beat with coloured
  duration bars growing from the left: THIS, Tiles (9 comps), without / AFTER
  EFFECTS, Chat card, Burst, CLAUDE WRITES THE SCRIPT, HUD, Soundtrack. In bar 8,
  32 keyframe diamonds pop on 32nd notes, and a red playhead sweeps across.
- Chyrons: a full-width band slams in from the left for two beats, then whips
  out right. Red "EVERY LAYER." on bar 7, yellow "EVERY KEYFRAME." on bar 8,
  ink 150 px type.

06 RENDER (bars 9-10), ink ground with a faint grid
- A film strip (dark tape, paper perforations) whips in from the right and
  scrolls left, accelerating at the end. Its ten frames are live, frozen moments
  of the earlier sections: ANYONE, the mosaic, AFTER EFFECTS struck out, the chat
  card, the burst, CLAUDE WRITES THE SCRIPT, the UI…
- "EVERY FRAME." (paper, 150 px) on bar 9. "RENDERED." (paper with red offset,
  170 px) on bar 10.
- A red progress bar fills, with a mono counter "RENDERING frame 0000 / 1200"
  counting up to "DONE 1200 / 1200 ✓ effectcraft-cli render" on bar 10 beat 3.

07 ANYONE CAN (bar 11), paper ground with a dot grid
- "ANYONE" (ink, 300 px) slams in. "CAN." (red, 300 px, ink offset) slams in a
  16th later.
- Beat 2: a red 12-point burst sticker pops beside the period and spins.
- Beat 3: the credit types in mono with a blinking red caret:
  "written by Claude · built & rendered in EffectCraft". End on this frame.
</structure>

<sound>
132 BPM, A minor. A kick on every beat, except: bar 3 drops beats 1 and 3,
bars 4-5 are half-time, and bar 11 has only the downbeat. Claps on 2 and 4.
Hats in bars 2 and 6-10 (16ths with open hats in 7-10). Soft 16th hats under
bars 4-5. An off-beat saw bass from bar 2. Chord stabs on section downbeats.
A plucked arpeggio under the typing. A sequenced pluck for the keyframe pops.
A snare roll into bar 11, and a final sustained chord.

Sound design: impacts on bars 2, 6, 7, 9 and 11 (biggest on 6 and 11), a hit
when AFTER EFFECTS slams in, a whoosh on the strike, blips on the dots, typing
clicks, a send click, a riser from the click into the red reveal, whooshes into
sections, blips as layers land, render ticks, and a riser through bar 10 into
the end title.

Voice-over: a calm, clear British female voice, each line placed on the grid:
0.10 s "Anyone can make" · 1.82 s "this." · 3.67 s "Without After Effects." ·
5.45 s "Just" · 6.36 s "describe it." · 9.10 s "Claude writes the script." ·
10.93 s "EffectCraft builds every layer," · 12.76 s "every keyframe," ·
14.57 s "and renders every frame." · 18.20 s "Anyone can."
Duck the music about 6 dB under the voice and let the kicks pump the bed.
</sound>

<build>
1. Build it inside EffectCraft with one After Effects-style script
   (effectcraft-cli script build.jsx --save-as anyone-can.ecproj): a Main comp,
   one precomp per section (each with its own background solid), and the HUD as
   two comps (marks recoloured with a Fill adjustment layer, plus the black tags).
2. Use real keyframes with velocity/influence eases for every move. Use
   expressions for the HUD, the typing (styled text with a red caret), the
   counters and the beat pulses. Turn on motion blur on moving layers. Feed the
   viewer and the film strip with time-remapped precomps of the real sections.
3. Make the soundtrack (music, sound design and voice) as one 48 kHz WAV and put
   it on an audio layer in Main.
4. Before the full render, render one frame per beat (44 frames) and look at
   them. Fix anything that overlaps, is hard to read or is off the beat.
5. Render from EffectCraft: effectcraft-cli render anyone-can.ecproj --comp Main
   --format h264 --bitrate 24000 --audio on.
</build>

<start>
Build it without asking anything. Give me the MP4 and the .ecproj.
</start>
```
